The Render deployment failure 'failed to read dockerfile: open Dockerfile: no such file or directory' is caused by Render looking for a Dockerfile in the root of the repository, but your Dockerfiles are located in the service subdirectories (e.g. `radio-agentic/services/automation/Dockerfile`). Please go to **Settings → Build & Deploy** in your Render Dashboard for your service, and set the **Dockerfile Path** to point to the correct file (e.g., `radio-agentic/services/automation/Dockerfile`).
